Privacy policy of the demo websites

Who we are

These demo websites are made by Rafael Quintaneiro, who is the data controller. The data protection officer (DPO) is Rafael Quintaneiro. Contact: WhatsApp +5584991732610.

What data appears

Each site shows only public information about the business itself: name, category, address, business phone, opening hours and links to the business's social media. It comes from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL license) and from the business's own public pages.

We do not collect data from visitors: there are no first-party cookies, forms, logins or tracking tools.

What we use it for

We use this data only to create a demo website and offer it to the business itself, with a single, honest message. Legal basis: legitimate interest (art. 7, IX, of Brazilian Law 13,709/2018, the LGPD), with the option to receive no further contact.

Third-party services

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and, when a visitor reaches the map, the OpenStreetMap map. The buttons for WhatsApp, Google Maps and social media open those services only when the visitor taps them. These services may receive the visitor's IP address, under their own policies.

The sites are hosted on a static page hosting service (Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages), which records technical access under its own policy.

Your rights and how to delete

The business may at any time ask for access, correction, objection or deletion of its data, and ask for the website to be deleted, by contacting WhatsApp +5584991732610. The website is deleted and the business is placed on a do-not-contact list (we keep only the identifier and the phone number, so as not to bother it again).

If the business does not reply, the demo website is deleted after 7 days.

How long we keep it

The list of businesses stays only on the computer of the person making the demo, until the end of the campaign. Nothing is sold or shared.

Translation

Versions in other languages are a courtesy. The Portuguese text prevails.
